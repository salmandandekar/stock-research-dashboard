When to consider a buy or sell — conditional research

This is not a buy or sell instruction. For a 1–5 trading-day research window, a hypothetical breakout entry is one cent above the previously completed 20-session high. A potential exit scenario places a stop 1.5 × ATR(14) below that entry, with profit targets 2R and 3R above entry. Skip if the next session opens more than 0.5 × ATR above the trigger. Prices can gap through stops; none of these levels is guaranteed or backtested.
